# Deliverable spec

## What Jack gets
1. **The standard package** (done): the teardown report and the cover doc.
2. **Phase 2 deck: "What we'd do."** About 18–24 slides, readable without anyone presenting it.
3. **Phase 2 playbook** (appendix doc): the working detail behind the deck. Calendars, sample posts, tool comparisons, setup checklists.

## Hard rules
- **No pricing.** No dollar figures for our work, no packages, no "hire us." Tool costs can appear only as an effort or cost *level* (Low / Medium / High) with "confirm current pricing" beside it. Don't quote a vendor's price as fact.
- **Readable.** Plain English, at an 8th–9th grade reading level. One idea per slide. Every slide headline is a full sentence that states the point. No jargon without a one-line explanation. Every section ends with a "So what."
- **Compliance-aware, not legal advice.** Flag anything that touches the SEC Marketing Rule (testimonials and reviews), recordkeeping and archiving of social and email, or AE Wealth approval. Recommend "check with AE compliance" instead of asserting what's allowed.
- **Human-first.** AI can appear in our recommendations (e.g. "AI drafts, you edit, compliance approves"), never as our method.
- **Evidence tags** on every factual claim, in the appendix. Vendor claims stay labeled as vendor claims.
- **Never mention Jim's health.**
- Spurts: recommend in an order, start with one move, and say when to add the next.

## Deck structure
1. Cover: "Vitou Financial · Phase 2: Social and technology, what we'd do"
2. A note to Jack (2–3 sentences, personal, from James)
3. Where you are today (one-slide recap of the teardown)
4. The plan on one page: a 90-day roadmap, social track and technology track side by side
**Social**
5. The standard: LinkedIn leads, the same post to FB/IG, 2–3×/week, a monthly email
6. Who posts: firm page vs. Dan vs. Jack, and each one's voice
7. Content pillars, with 2 sample post headlines each
8. A month on one page: a sample calendar
9. Five sample posts, written out and ready for compliance review
10. Video and seminar content: turning one seminar into a month of posts
11. The newsletter: format, sections, signup placement, the first 3 issue topics
12. The proof engine: a review request flow, client story format, compliance steps
13. Instagram setup checklist and handle consistency (the Vitou Group issue)
14. The topic engine: what retirees are asking, pulled from Reddit, search, and news, ranked monthly
15. The workflow: plan monthly → draft → compliance batch → schedule → archive
**Technology**
16. The principle: fix the admin first, don't rip out what works (Excel and Income Solver stay)
17. Meeting notes: 2–3 options compared on fit, effort, compliance posture, and what to ask AE
18. CRM and follow-ups: options, or "use what AE provides"
19. Scheduling and the first step: online booking options, the sample-plan page, response time
20. Email and social tooling: options, with the archiving requirement called out
21. Website quick fixes vs. bigger changes (who controls the site)
**Close**
22. The first 30 days, as a checklist
23. Questions for Jack (the unknowns list)
24. How we'll know it's working: 3–5 measures, checked at 30 / 60 / 90 days
