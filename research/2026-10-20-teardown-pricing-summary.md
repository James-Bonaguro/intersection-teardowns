# Teardown pricing: summary and decision

**Internal only.** Never copy anything from this file into client-facing work.
Source: `2026-10-20-teardown-pricing-market-scan.html` (market scan, prices checked 2026-10-20).

## How to read the numbers
- Every price in the scan is a **published list price**, not a confirmed sale. Tag them `observed (published)`, never `RECHECKED`.
- No seller's revenue or order counts could be verified. Supply is proven; demand is not.

## What the scan found
- **Dead zone:** a $750 to $1,500 standalone written report sold cold to local businesses. No successful named example was found. `inferred`
- **Two shapes that do sell:**
  1. A fast productized video teardown, about $250 to $550. Example: Pages.Report, $299, 15-minute private video, delivered in 48 hours. `observed (published)`
  2. A paid diagnostic credited toward implementation. Example: MarginFix, $1,450 / $2,950, with 100% of the fee credited to implementation booked within 30 days. `observed (published)`
- **Financial advisors:** both advisor-specific audits found (Indigo, WealthReach) are free lead magnets. No paid standalone audit sold to advisors was found. `observed (published)`
- Kitces research, cited by Twenty Over Ten, puts advisor hard-dollar marketing at 1 to 2% of revenue, so about $5k to $10k a year for a $500k-revenue firm. `observed (published)`
- A $1,450 to $2,950 audit would therefore take roughly **15 to 59%** of that budget (corrected figure, not 15 to 50%). `est.` / `inferred`

## Decision
**We do not sell teardowns.** Teardowns are portfolio proof and a door-opener for other work. No pricing or sales call to action appears in any client-facing file.

## Internal note (never client-facing)
If we ever charge, the evidence points to the credited-diagnostic shape: the fee credits in full toward follow-on work.
