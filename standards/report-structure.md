# Report structure

Drafts, the final markdown, and the design template all use this order. Section numbers on the page shift if an optional section is off.

| # | Section | Must contain |
|---|---|---|
| — | Cover | Company, prepared for, date, version, public-evidence disclaimer. |
| 01 | The verdict | Headline. Bottom line (≤90 words). Scorecard: 6 rows, STRONG / MIXED / WEAK, one-line evidence each. 3 things to know. Optional personal note. |
| 02 | What's working | 2–3 hard numbers. Credit where due. |
| 03 | Online proof | Reviews table: source, count, rating, latest, what a prospect notices. |
| 04 | Social & email | Channel table: channel, status, what a prospect sees. |
| 05 | Social is the front door | The social standard applied to this company, with 2–3 `PUBLISHED` stats. |
| 06 | The first step | Visitor journey in 4 steps. Issue register: HIGH / MED / LOW. |
| 07 | The biggest opening | Today vs after the moves, 4–5 rows. |
| 08 | The moves | 3–4 ranked moves, each with a MEASURE line and timeframe. "This week" quick-fix checklist. |
| 09 | Beyond marketing | Optional. One operational project worth scoping. |
| 10 | Limits | Every `UNKNOWN`. |
| — | Appendix | Numbered sources: description, URL, tag, date. |

- Every section ends with one **So what** sentence.
- Footnote numbers in the text match the appendix.
- **Competitor** teardowns: 02–07 describe the rival; 08 becomes "How you win against them".
