# Social standard

Social media is the front door. It's where buyers find a company, vet it, and decide whether it has authority. Every teardown grades it.

## The standard

| Channel | Cadence | Role |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 2–3× a week | Leads. Company page **and** the principals personally. |
| Facebook | Same post | Cross-post the LinkedIn post. |
| Instagram | Same post | Cross-post. Missing account = finding; recommend creating it. |
| Email newsletter | Monthly | The month's best posts to the whole list. AI-drafted, human-reviewed (and compliance, if regulated). |

- Website link in **every profile**, not every post.
- **Content mix:** education, commentary on the week's news, analysis of one rule/rate/trend, proof (recaps, client stories with sign-off).
- **Runs on a schedule:** a month planned in one sitting, approved in one batch, scheduled through an approved tool.
- **Topic sourcing:** a scraping setup (Firecrawl or similar, into a spreadsheet) ranks what buyers are asking each month in forums, search, and the news. The client picks and writes the take.

## Grading

Each channel gets one status: `ACTIVE` (meets cadence) · `THIN` (exists, below cadence or incomplete) · `MISSING` · `NOT ASSESSED` (no access).
