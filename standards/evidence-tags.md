# Evidence tags

| Tag | Meaning | Can it carry a headline or a move? |
|---|---|---|
| `RECHECKED` | Re-verified on the live source during fact-check, dated. | Yes |
| `PUBLISHED` | Third-party study or industry stat, cited as reported, with its year. | Yes |
| `OBSERVED` | Seen during research or from a screenshot, linked and dated, not rechecked. | No, supporting detail only |
| `est.` | Arithmetic on public numbers. Show the inputs. | Only labelled "est." |
| `INFERRED` | Reasoned from tagged facts. Name what it rests on. | Only if the reasoning is shown |
| `UNKNOWN` | Not visible from outside. | No. Goes in Limits. |

Every fact in the appendix has: number, description, URL, tag, date.
