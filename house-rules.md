# House rules

1. **Public evidence only.** No form submissions, bookings, or staff contact without the client's approval. Anything behind a login is `UNKNOWN` unless the client shares it.
2. **Never present AI as our method.** Don't say AI did the research or writing. Recommending AI tools *to the client* is fine ("AI drafts the newsletter; you and compliance review it").
3. **No fees in the report.** Verified, published competitor prices may be cited as evidence. No speculative estimates.
4. **Tag every fact** per `evidence-tags.md`. Agreement between two drafts is never corroboration.
5. **Recommend in spurts.** Rank the moves. Lead with one. The next one starts once the first is running.
6. **Fit the client.** Every recommendation must be something this client can actually do with the people and rules they have.
7. **Fair, not harsh.** Say what's working first. Where friction is probably compliance or a franchise rule, say so.
8. **Voice.** Peer to peer, plain, complete sentences. Write to the person ("you"), not about them. Never salesy.
