02 / BUSINESS MODEL

Revenue is concentrated in two enterprise customers.

[Company] sells annual SaaS contracts to mid-market logistics operators, priced per vehicle per month. Most revenue comes from subscriptions, with a smaller share from onboarding and integration fees.

Two enterprise accounts, signed in [year], now make up an estimated 38% of revenue.1 Both contracts come up for renewal within the next 18 months. Public case studies suggest both are expanding usage, which lowers the near-term risk but not the structural one.

The long tail of roughly [n] smaller customers is healthy. Reviews cite ease of setup and support quality, and job postings point to a self-serve tier in development.
