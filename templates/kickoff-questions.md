# Kickoff questions (15 minutes)

1. What decision should this help you make in the next 90 days?
2. Who's your best customer, and how did the last three find you?
3. Self or competitor? If competitor, which one keeps you up at night?
4. Every social account, including personal LinkedIn for the principals.
5. Can you share LinkedIn page analytics (a screenshot is fine)?
6. Is there an email list? Roughly how big, and when did it last get an email?
7. Who has to approve marketing (compliance, franchisor, partner)?
8. What tools does the team run on today?
9. What's off limits or already in progress?
