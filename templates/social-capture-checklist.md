# Social capture checklist (about 10 minutes per prospect)

Take screenshots, not screen recordings. Name the files `company-channel-1.png`, drop them in the files section, and say "add socials."

## The standard we grade against
LinkedIn first, then the same post shared to Facebook and Instagram. Cross-posting is fine. What matters: real value on a steady rhythm, and the website linked in every profile (not every post).

## Before you start
- [ ] Turn on LinkedIn private viewing mode if you don't want them to see you looked
- [ ] Use your dedicated teardown inbox for any signups

## Capture
- [ ] **Instagram:** profile header (followers, posts, bio, link), first two rows of the grid, date of the latest post
- [ ] **LinkedIn company page:** followers, employee count, latest 3 posts with dates, Jobs tab
- [ ] **LinkedIn personal (owner + key people):** headline, About, recent activity
- [ ] **Facebook page:** followers, latest 3 posts with dates and engagement
- [ ] **For every channel:** does the profile or bio link to the website?
- [ ] **No LinkedIn access?** Ask the prospect to forward a screenshot of their page analytics, or mark it "Not assessed"
- [ ] **YouTube / TikTok:** subscribers, latest video date (only if they exist)
- [ ] **Meta Ad Library** (logged in): search the company name and screenshot any active ads
- [ ] **Newsletter:** subscribe 2–3 weeks before sending the teardown, then forward or screenshot what arrives. No signup on the site = a finding

## What I'll do with it
Each item gets a status (Active / Thin / Missing / Not assessed), lands on the Social & Email page, and is cited in the sources as "observed from screenshot, [date]."
